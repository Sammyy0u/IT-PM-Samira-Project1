**Project Title:** Online Book Rental Subscription System  

## 1. Project Overview

BookLoop is an online platform that allows users to rent physical books through a monthly membership subscription.

Instead of purchasing every book individually, customers can choose books from an online catalog, select a membership plan, receive books at home, and return them after reading.

BookLoop uses a book exchange service in which the courier delivers the next set of books and collects the previously rented books at the same time.

The platform aims to make reading more affordable, convenient, and accessible.

## 2. Problem Statement

Purchasing books regularly can be expensive for students and frequent readers. Traditional libraries may also require physical visits and may not provide a convenient home delivery service.

BookLoop addresses these problems by providing an online catalog, flexible membership plans, home delivery, and a convenient book exchange process.

## 3. Project Objectives

- Design an online platform for physical book rentals.
- Define membership plans with different rental limits.
- Enable users to browse and search for books.
- Manage book availability and rental orders.
- Coordinate book deliveries and simultaneous returns.
- Track rental periods, memberships, and order statuses.
- Provide administrators with tools to manage inventory and customers.

## 4. Target Audience

- University and school students.
- Regular readers and book enthusiasts.
- Customers who prefer renting books instead of buying them.
- Readers who value convenient home delivery.

## 5. Proposed Membership Plans

| Feature | Basic | Standard | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Books at a time | Up to 2 | Up to 3 | Up to 4 |
| Monthly exchanges | Up to 2 | Up to 3 | Up to 4 |
| Proposed rental period | 14 days | 21 days | 30 days |
| Main benefit | Affordable access | More book choices | Greater flexibility |

These are preliminary business rules and may be adjusted during requirements analysis.

## 6. Main System Features

1. User registration and login.
2. Membership selection and management.
3. Book catalog browsing.
4. Search and filtering by title, author, and genre.
5. Book availability checking.
6. Rental order creation.
7. Delivery scheduling and tracking.
8. Simultaneous delivery and return management.
9. Rental history and due-date reminders.
10. Administrative inventory and membership management.

## 7. Main Business Process

1. A customer creates an account.
2. The customer selects and activates a membership plan.
3. The customer browses the catalog and selects available books.
4. The system checks the membership limits and book availability.
5. The customer places a rental order.
6. The administrator or warehouse staff prepares the books.
7. The courier delivers the new books and collects the previous books, when applicable.
8. The system updates delivery, return, and inventory statuses.
9. The customer can place another order according to the membership rules.

## 8. Project Deliverables

The team will prepare the following six core deliverables:

1. **Project Plan:** Project background, objectives, scope, and target audience.
2. **Contract / Agreement:** Team responsibilities, project expectations, and collaboration rules.
3. **Use Case Modeling:** Use Case Diagram and detailed Use Case Specifications.
4. **Business Process Model (BPM):** Visual representation of the rental and book exchange workflow.
5. **Work Breakdown Structure (WBS):** Hierarchical decomposition of project activities.
6. **Gantt Chart:** Project schedule, milestones, task dependencies, and deadlines.

The deliverables will be incorporated into the final report and presentation.

## 9. Project Scope

**In Scope**
- Web-based platform planning.
- Membership and rental management.
- Book catalog and availability.
- Order and delivery management.
- Book return and inventory tracking.
- Administrative functions.

**Out of Scope**
